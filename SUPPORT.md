# Support

## Current support posture

Viable is pre-release software. The source may be evaluated, built, modified, and used under the repository license, but MythologIQ Labs, LLC does not currently represent Viable as a supported production product and does not provide a service-level agreement for this repository.

Public source visibility is intentionally separate from supported product release readiness.

## Where to ask for help

For non-sensitive questions about:

- building the repository;
- reproducing a bug;
- understanding documented behavior;
- proposing a bounded improvement;
- clarifying a current limitation;

use GitHub issues and include enough context to reproduce or understand the problem.

Before filing an issue, review:

- [`README.md`](README.md)
- [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md)
- [`docs/status/current-state.md`](docs/status/current-state.md)
- existing open and closed issues.

## What is not currently promised

The repository does not currently promise:

- production support;
- uptime or hosted-service availability;
- a fixed issue-response time;
- a fixed vulnerability-remediation time;
- signed installers;
- automatic updates or rollback support;
- backward-compatible workspace migrations;
- backup and restore guarantees;
- support for every operating system;
- live Webdog, Context.dev, ViMax, social, analytics, CRM, or publishing integrations;
- migration or support for third-party forks.

Those boundaries may change as release-foundation work is completed and an explicit supported release policy is adopted.

## Security issues

Do not use a public support issue for a suspected vulnerability, secret, credential exposure, or sensitive-data finding. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Commercial support

The Apache-2.0 source license does not require MythologIQ Labs to provide support, hosting, warranties, or managed services. MythologIQ Labs may choose to offer commercial support, hosting, customization, packaged distributions, or other services in the future under separately stated terms.
