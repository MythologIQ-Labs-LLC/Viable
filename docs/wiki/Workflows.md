# Workflows

Viable is organized around connected operating workflows rather than isolated features.

## Product and ICP

Establish product truth, capture evidence, compare ICP hypotheses, record contradictions and disqualifiers, and choose the next validation action.

## Signals and Market

Collect or import bounded external evidence, preserve source health and provenance, review signals, tag and assign them, and convert reviewed evidence into proposed work.

## Website Watch

Stage 1 supports watched-site/target records and strict Webdog-compatible manual evidence import. Live crawling and live Webdog/Context.dev synchronization are not currently claimed.

## Campaigns and Studio

Create campaign intent from approved claims and reviewed evidence, produce canonical assets, derive destination variants, review them by name, and export manual packages when approval gates pass.

## Repository Growth

Assess public repository readiness across product clarity, trust, discoverability, time to value, releases, community readiness, adoption evidence, sustainability, and commercial path. Build owned action plans and governed launch rooms without manufacturing engagement.

## Video Production

Create provider-neutral video briefs and production packages, preserve rights and consent context, import render artifacts, and require Viable-owned review. ViMax remains an optional compatibility target rather than a mandatory runtime.

## Calendar and Activation

Plan timing and destinations, bind approval to exact source versions, export credential-free manual activation packages, and record actual delivery/failure evidence separately from scheduling.

## Analytics and Learning

Capture baselines, explicit metric states, observation windows, retrospectives, attribution uncertainty, and one reversible next action.
