# Product Model

Viable treats marketability as a governed system rather than a pile of disconnected marketing tools.

## Product Core

Product Core owns canonical product truth, including capabilities, limitations, evidence-backed claims, positioning, offers, and ICP hypotheses.

Downstream workflows may contribute evidence, but they may not silently rewrite Product Core authority.

## ICP discovery

Viable supports multiple ICP hypotheses rather than forcing an early persona into permanent truth. Hypotheses may include user, buyer, decision-maker, approver, influencer, champion, blocker, partner, maintainer, contributor, and disqualifier roles.

Evidence, assumptions, contradictions, freshness, confidence, experiments, and revision history remain explicit.

## Signals and Market

Signals represent externally observed or imported evidence. They preserve source health, provenance, review state, limitations, and failure semantics. A failed source is not treated as an empty market.

## Campaigns and Studio

Campaigns consume approved Product Core claims and reviewed evidence. Canonical assets remain distinct from destination/channel variants. Generated content begins as a suggestion or draft and does not approve itself.

## External action

Scheduling, approval, export, delivery, and successful outcome are separate states. Named human review is required for consequential external action.

## Learning

Retrospectives can propose changes to product assumptions, ICP confidence, positioning, offers, and channels, but learning does not silently mutate canonical product truth.

For the governing product definition, see the [Product Requirements Document](https://github.com/MythologIQ-Labs-LLC/Viable/blob/main/docs/product/PRD.md) and [marketability operating model](https://github.com/MythologIQ-Labs-LLC/Viable/blob/main/docs/product/marketability-operating-model.md).
