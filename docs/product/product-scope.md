# Viable Product Scope

## Product definition

Viable is a local-first marketability operating system for products, public repositories, founders, maintainers, and small product teams.

It helps a founder or team establish product truth, identify and validate the proper ideal customer profile, determine whether a product is ready to market, identify the most valuable opportunity, create evidence-backed campaigns and assets, coordinate approved distribution, capture qualified demand, support sales conversations, and learn from outcomes.

## Core product domains

### Product truth

- capabilities and limitations;
- lifecycle, release, and supported environments;
- claims and proof;
- positioning and alternatives;
- pricing, packaging, offers, and calls to action;
- brand voice and terminology;
- asset inventory and maturity.

### ICP discovery and validation

- multiple candidate ICP hypotheses;
- users, economic buyers, decision-makers, approvers, influencers, champions, blockers, partners, maintainers, contributors, and disqualifiers;
- problem intensity and urgency;
- product fit and time to value;
- access and reachable channels;
- proof and evidence quality;
- adoption friction and buying constraints;
- commercial viability and retention potential;
- anti-ICP and disqualification conditions;
- explicit assumptions, contradictions, confidence, freshness, and owner;
- validation experiments;
- selected, secondary, adjacent, rejected, and historical ICP candidates;
- revision history and evidence that would change the selected ICP.

### Marketability assessment

- transparent readiness dimensions;
- product truth, ICP clarity, audience clarity, urgency, positioning, offer, proof, discoverability, content, distribution, conversion, sales, and measurement readiness;
- supporting evidence and confidence;
- prioritized gaps;
- recommended actions and validation experiments;
- ownership, effort, dependency, and verification.

### Market intelligence

- event signals;
- social and community signals;
- search and website signals;
- competitor and category signals;
- public repository signals;
- customer, support, adoption, retention, and sales feedback;
- research inbox and evidence relationships;
- signals that can strengthen, weaken, or challenge an ICP hypothesis without silently rewriting it.

### Campaign strategy

- ICP, audience, and outcome;
- problem, trigger, offer, and message;
- proof and call to action;
- channel strategy;
- asset matrix;
- schedule and measurement;
- review and approval.

### Content and creative production

- website and search content;
- social content;
- articles, guides, comparisons, FAQs, and case studies;
- email and outreach drafts;
- sales enablement assets;
- image and carousel briefs;
- short-video briefs, scripts, storyboards, and production packages;
- review, versioning, approval, and asset history.

### Activation

- editorial and campaign calendar;
- manual exports;
- approved platform adapters;
- publication and delivery evidence;
- lead capture;
- follow-up queues;
- public repository launch rooms.

### Leads and sales support

- people and organizations;
- source and consent;
- ICP fit and qualification;
- lifecycle and opportunity stage;
- account research;
- meeting preparation;
- objection handling;
- follow-up and proposals;
- outcome and learning capture.

### Measurement and learning

- campaign and asset performance;
- website and search outcomes;
- public repository growth;
- lead and opportunity influence;
- product adoption and retention;
- ICP confidence and revision evidence;
- experiments;
- attribution with stated uncertainty;
- retrospectives and learning ledger.

## Integration scope

Viable may integrate with:

- websites and content management systems;
- analytics and search platforms;
- social research and publishing platforms;
- public event providers;
- GitHub and package ecosystems;
- email, newsletter, booking, and CRM systems;
- design, image, audio, and video tools;
- local model and automation runtimes.

Integrations remain adapters. They do not own canonical product, ICP, campaign, evidence, approval, identity, lead, or asset records.

## Explicit non-goals

Viable is not:

- an autonomous social persona;
- a bulk messaging or spam platform;
- a fake-engagement or paid-star system;
- a general-purpose CRM replacement;
- a social listening surveillance product;
- a guarantee of a correct ICP, search ranking, GitHub Trending, virality, leads, or sales without evidence;
- a compliance control plane;
- a replacement for legal, privacy, advertising, accessibility, or platform-policy review;
- a reason to bypass supported APIs, access controls, consent, or licensing;
- a system that treats a broad audience, follower, event attendee, repository visitor, or anonymous interaction as a qualified customer by default.

## Product authority

Viable owns:

- the product model;
- ICP hypotheses and their evidence-backed lifecycle;
- marketability assessment;
- evidence and provenance;
- campaign intent;
- canonical assets and variants;
- claims and proof relationships;
- review and approval state;
- lead and relationship context;
- measurement and learning records.

External providers own their APIs, accounts, delivery systems, platform responses, and service availability.

## Success criteria

Viable is successful when a user can answer with evidence:

1. What is true about the product?
2. Who is the proper ICP and why is it stronger than the alternatives?
3. Which ICP assumptions remain uncertain or contradicted?
4. Who should be explicitly excluded or deprioritized?
5. Is the product ready to market?
6. What is preventing discovery, understanding, trust, evaluation, adoption, or purchase?
7. Which audience and opportunity deserve attention now?
8. What should be created, improved, published, tested, or discussed next?
9. What requires human approval?
10. What produced qualified interest, sales progress, adoption, contribution, or product learning?
11. What should change in the product, ICP, message, offer, or channels because of the observed results?
