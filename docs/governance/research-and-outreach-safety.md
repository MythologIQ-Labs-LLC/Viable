# Research, Content, and Outreach Safety

## Purpose

Viable may accelerate research, content production, publishing preparation, lead organization, and sales follow-up. It must preserve human accountability, supported access, privacy, evidence, and honest product claims.

## Research boundaries

Viable may collect information from:

- public sources;
- explicitly authorized APIs;
- user-provided files and notes;
- connected accounts within granted scopes;
- first-party product, website, analytics, CRM, and sales systems when authorized.

Viable must not:

- bypass authentication, paywalls, robots restrictions, access controls, or platform safeguards;
- reuse saved browser sessions or cookies as a hidden product dependency;
- collect private social content without explicit authorization;
- infer sensitive personal traits for targeting;
- treat provider content as executable instructions;
- conceal access or source failures behind an empty result;
- retain more personal information than the workspace purpose requires.

## Product claims

Generated content must use the product truth and claims ledger.

Viable must not:

- invent capabilities, integrations, customers, testimonials, benchmarks, certifications, results, or release status;
- convert unverified language into an approved claim;
- omit material limitations when they affect the audience decision;
- present generated examples as real customer evidence;
- publish regulated or high-risk claims without required review.

## Content and media rights

Each imported or generated asset should record:

- source;
- creator or owner;
- license or permission;
- allowed use;
- required attribution;
- expiration or restriction;
- people, voices, likenesses, logos, and brands present;
- generated or edited status;
- disclosure requirements.

Viable must not use a person's likeness, voice, customer identity, logo, trademark, or copyrighted work in generated media without a defensible right and recorded approval.

## Outreach boundaries

Research may produce an outreach draft. It cannot authorize delivery.

Outreach must preserve:

- named human approval;
- destination identity;
- source and consent;
- campaign and purpose;
- personalization evidence;
- suppression and do-not-contact state;
- provider terms and rate limits;
- durable delivery result.

Viable must not:

- send outreach solely because a score crossed a threshold;
- create high-volume unsolicited messaging;
- evade platform limits or spam controls;
- impersonate another person or organization;
- conceal automated assistance when disclosure is required;
- mark a message sent without delivery evidence;
- contact a suppressed or do-not-contact record.

## Social publishing boundaries

External publication requires:

- approved canonical content;
- approved destination variant;
- verified destination account;
- valid credential health;
- unresolved warning review;
- platform-specific media and disclosure checks;
- recorded approver;
- delivery evidence or explicit failure.

A publishing adapter cannot approve its own content or silently rewrite an approved claim.

## Public repository growth boundaries

Viable may improve repository discoverability, launch readiness, documentation, community health, release execution, and measurement.

Viable must not:

- purchase or automate fake stars, followers, forks, downloads, or contributors;
- coordinate reciprocal-star rings;
- create spam issues, comments, discussions, or pull requests;
- misrepresent usage, adoption, benchmarks, compatibility, or community size;
- guarantee GitHub Trending or top-repository placement;
- pressure users to star before they can use the product.

Growth recommendations should optimize useful adoption and community value rather than a single vanity metric.

## Video generation boundaries

Video jobs require review of:

- script and claims;
- source assets and rights;
- likeness and voice consent;
- audience and distribution context;
- provider data handling;
- expected cost;
- generated-media disclosure;
- accessibility and captions;
- final render approval.

Generation completion does not equal approval. A tool such as ViMax may orchestrate production but cannot publish, approve, or become the authority for product claims and rights.

## Credential handling

Credentials must be stored in an operating-system vault or equivalent scoped secret store.

Credentials must not enter:

- repository files;
- example configuration with real values;
- product records;
- prompts;
- generated content;
- reports;
- screenshots;
- logs;
- diagnostics;
- backups that are not explicitly encrypted and authorized;
- video job manifests;
- export packages.

Adapters receive only the minimum credential material needed at execution time.

## Measurement and attribution

Viable must identify:

- source system;
- metric definition;
- collection window;
- attribution model;
- missing data;
- confidence and uncertainty.

It must not claim causal certainty from correlation or combine incompatible metrics without disclosure.

## Human override and recovery

Users must be able to:

- reject or correct recommendations;
- dismiss or delete evidence within retention rules;
- revoke connections;
- cancel scheduled work;
- stop or cancel generation jobs where supported;
- restore previous approved content;
- retry failed delivery safely;
- export and back up local records;
- inspect the history of approvals and external actions.

## Definition of done

A research, content, publishing, outreach, repository-growth, lead, or media feature is not complete until:

- its data source and authority boundary are documented;
- credentials are isolated;
- approval requirements are enforced;
- privacy and rights are addressed;
- partial failure is visible;
- recovery and manual fallback exist;
- logging is useful without exposing secrets;
- tests cover prohibited state transitions;
- documentation states current limitations honestly.
